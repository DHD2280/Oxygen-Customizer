        Made for OxygenOS, not ported to it.

        Most of what Oxygen Customizer changes only exists on OnePlus, OPPO and realme phones: the Now Bar, the Plus Key, Pixelworks display chips and the OnePlus Launcher. These are the parts you will not find in a generic Android customizer.

        Lockscreen

        Now Bar and Peek notifications

        Turn the space between the lockscreen shortcuts into a live bar for music, battery, weather or your latest notification. Tap the album art to open an extended player over blurred artwork. Peek notifications add compact cards to the top or bottom of the lockscreen.

        Always On Display

        Edge light

        Light up the screen edges when a notification arrives while the display sleeps, and pulse again until you read it.

        Quick Settings

        Separate QS, laid out by you

        Split Quick Settings from notifications, then place the media player, two highlight tiles and the brightness and volume sliders where you want them. Add widgets for weather, photos, media, Wallet or Home controls.

        Buttons and gestures

        A Plus Key that does what you want

        Give the Plus Key its own action for single, double, triple and long press, with a separate set while the screen is off. Hold the gesture bar for Circle to Search, add swipe-up shortcuts to the bottom corners, and use the volume keys to wake the phone, skip tracks or toggle the torch.

        OnePlus Launcher

        Shelf, themed icons, real grids

        Swap Discover for Shelf, force themed icons on home, drawer, folders, search and the taskbar, and set your own grid for each.

        Display

        Pixelworks and Pocket Studio

        Turn on MEMC and SDR to HDR for the apps and activities you pick, force-enable Pocket Studio and apply App Lag Fix to single apps.

        Music

        Smart Pause and Fluid Music

        Pause playback when you mute and resume it if you turn the sound back on in time. Choose which apps get Fluid Music, and run the Pulse visualizer on the navbar, lockscreen or AOD.

        And the small things

        Red One clock
        Recolor or turn off the red 1 in the OnePlus clock, on the lockscreen and in Quick Settings.
        Statusbar logo
        Put one of 33 logos, or your own image, next to the clock.
        Fingerprint icon
        Pick from 60 presets or use your own image, scaled to fit the sensor.
        Sleep on flat
        Turn the screen off when the phone lies face up, unless an app is keeping it awake.
        Quieter shade
        Hide the charging, developer mode, flashlight and low battery notifications.
        Advanced reboot
        Restart to recovery, bootloader or safe mode, behind a fingerprint or face check.
        OTA card image
        Replace the picture on the system update screen with your own.
        Backup and restore
        Export every setting to one file and bring it back after a clean flash.

        Set up in five steps.

        You need root through Magisk, KernelSU or APatch, plus an LSPosed build that matches your OxygenOS version. Other root or hook methods will not work.

        Nightly builds
        1. Install LSPosed for your OxygenOS version, then download and install Oxygen Customizer.
        2. Enable the Oxygen Customizer module in the LSPosed manager.
        3. Open the app and grant root access when Magisk asks.
        4. Follow the setup screens and wait while it builds a module for your exact ROM.
        5. Reboot when prompted. Your changes apply from the next boot.

        On OxygenOS 15 .850 or later, bug reports from any other LSPosed build are closed. Download modules only from their official pages.

        KernelSU 3.0 and meta modules

        KernelSU 3.0 no longer mounts modules by itself. Install Mountify (recommended), Magic Mount or Hybrid Mount. Magic Mount needs no setup. For Mountify, open its web UI and set: 

        mountify_mount = 2
mount_device_name = KSU
mountify_custom_umount = 2

        Use mountify_custom_umount = 1 with susfs older than 2.0.0. Reboot, then install Oxygen Customizer.

        Before you ask in the group.

        Does it need root?

        Yes. Oxygen Customizer hooks System UI through LSPosed and installs a small module, so it needs Magisk, KernelSU or APatch.

        Which phones and ROMs does it support?

        It targets OxygenOS 13 to 16 and ROMs based on it. ColorOS and realme UI share most of the code and mostly work, but they are not guaranteed.

        My phone bootloops after installing. What now?

        Boot into Safe Mode and remove the module. On KernelSU and its forks, follow the rescue guide.

        Can I use it with other customization apps?

        Yes, but two apps changing the same part of the UI can conflict. If something looks wrong, turn off the overlapping option in one of them.

        What is the difference between release and nightly builds?

        Release builds are optimized for everyday use. Nightly builds come from every commit and include the newest fixes along with extra logging for debugging.

        How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

        Update to the latest nightly first. Then open an issue with the Bug Report or Feature Request template and include your ROM, LSPosed build, steps to reproduce and logs.
